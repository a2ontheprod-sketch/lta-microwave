LTA MICROWAVE — 9 PRESETS & LIVE ANIMATIONS

The glass-window oscilloscope uses audio samples captured by the processor.
The display responds to the host parameter state. A timer drives animation at 30fps.

DISTORT: tanh saturation; waveform becomes sharply limited.
OVERCOOK: clipping and quantisation; waveform becomes squared/stepped;
          short bright overheating flashes.
WARP: slow modulated delay / chorus; waveform ripples and leans sideways.
GLITCH: rhythmic resampling stutter; horizontal interference strips flicker.
DEFROST: low-pass filtering and flutter; window shifts to ice-blue with fog.
BASS: low harmonic enhancement; concentric bass pulses fill the chamber.
REHEAT: feedback delay; faint shifted waveform trail appears.
STEAM: diffusion-like dual-feedback reverb; steam rises from the plate.
COMPRESS: peak-tracking dynamic range compression; scope amplitude tightens.

POWER: physical dial + ring meters show the amount of each active effect.
MIX: LCD text (drag or scroll to change) blends dry and processed audio.
START: microwave emits light and rotating platter; audio effects active.
STOP: goes dark and audio is dry bypass; effects remain selected.

The glass turns orange while cooking and shows a real scope signal.
The animated platter rotates and heating coils glow with live signal level.
Each button flashes on press and has an active red LED.

Visual UI is derived from original commissioned/generated artwork with a
repainted live LCD and a real oscilloscope overlay. No separate FX widget
is shown outside the microwave, and every audio control is on the appliance.
